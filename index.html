01 / The philosophy

Space · Listening · Presence · Time

Travel can ask
less of you and
give more back.

SILENCE is an invitation to meet a place without rushing through it.

We bring a small circle of mental health professionals together in Brazil. A thoughtful host, a considered place, and open time make room for conversation to find its own pace.

What SILENCE is not

Not a clinical programme. Not a checklist of sights. Not a schedule designed to keep you busy. This is travel, held with care.

    07 / A few clear answers

    Before you ask.

    Is SILENCE a professional training programme?

    No. SILENCE is a curated travel experience. There are no lectures, clinical sessions, or continuing education credits.

    Who is invited to join?

    Psychologists, psychotherapists, psychoanalysts, and psychiatrists interested in a thoughtful, small-group journey in Brazil.

    How many people travel together?

    Each journey is designed for a group of eight to ten guests.

    How much of the day is planned?

    Very little. There are optional moments to gather, alongside generous unscheduled time. Joining an activity is always a choice.

    What is included in the price?

    The full meal plan, ground transport, and inclusions are shared transparently with each invitation before a booking is confirmed.
